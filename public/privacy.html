Privacy Policy

THE GAME THAT FORGOT YOU — last updated November 2026

Short version

The notebook forgets things. We don't keep anything. This game collects no personal data, shows no ads, and contains no trackers or analytics of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score. The deepest page you've reached in the endless mode is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It never leaves it.
  • Sound and display settings (mute state) also live in local storage.

Clearing the game's site data, or uninstalling the app, erases all of it permanently.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal details.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location. The game needs no special permissions to play.

Children

The game contains no violence beyond an ink scribble being politely erased, no purchases, and no data collection — it is safe for readers of every age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date.
