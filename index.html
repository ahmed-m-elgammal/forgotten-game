THE GAME THAT FORGOT YOU.

The world forgets its rules — gravity, solidity, the ground itself. You have a little memory, a few paper clips, and one small scribble of a hero. Keep what matters. Reach the door marked OUT?.

ENTER to begin · ←/→ or A/D move · SPACE jump · 1–7 select a rule · ENTER keeps it · C sketch · sound starts after your first key or click
